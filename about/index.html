Research Experience

2025.02 - 2025.05

Adaptive Contrastive Optimization for Safe and Robust Robot Manipulation

  • Proposed ACORN, an adaptive contrastive optimization algorithm for robust and safe embodied intelligence deployment.
  • Used dual-perturbation contrastive learning to generate negative samples and align robot trajectories with expert demonstrations without manual annotation.
  • Designed safety-centered metrics to quantify manipulation stability under extreme conditions.
2025.09 - 2025.12

Equivariant Machine Learning Force Fields and Generative Materials Design

  • Studied diffusion models and flow matching for materials generation, with emphasis on energy-guided vector field optimization.
  • Explored integrating three-body attention into equivariant frameworks for stronger representation of complex atomic systems.
  • Investigated low-rank decomposition and related acceleration techniques for large-scale materials pretraining.

Selected Projects

2023.03 - 2024.03

Multimodal Biomedical Pattern Mining for Disease Combination Medication

  • Integrated multimodal drug data, including chemical structural groups, enzyme genes, and target genes.
  • Formulated joint medication pattern mining as a machine learning classification problem.
  • Built and deployed a Streamlit-based web system for remote access.
2024.02 - 2024.06

Intelligent Manhole Cover Hazard Recognition and Processing System

  • Applied object detection to recognize manhole cover status automatically.
  • Used diffusion-based image generation to mitigate sample imbalance.
  • Developed the application with UniApp for hazard recognition and processing workflows.
2024.12 - 2025.03

Macrosomia Risk Prediction with Ensemble Learning

  • Built an end-to-end risk prediction workflow based on ensemble learning.
  • Designed a multi-method feature selection strategy and a Stacking ensemble architecture.
  • Improved model generalization and stability for clinical risk prediction.
