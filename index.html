About Me

I’m Zhongquan Zhou, a Computer Science and Technology graduate student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My interests include machine learning, embodied intelligence, multimodal biomedical data mining, and software systems.

🎓 Education

  • Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2025-2028)
    • M.S. student in Computer Science and Technology
    • GPA: 3.70/4.0
  • Lanzhou University (2021-2025)
    • B.S. in Computer Science and Technology
    • GPA: 3.97/5.0

💻 Technical Skills

  • Programming Languages: Python, C++, JavaScript
  • Machine Learning: PyTorch, ensemble learning, contrastive learning, diffusion models
  • Systems & Tools: Linux, Git, Streamlit, UniApp
  • Areas of Interest:
    • Machine Learning
    • Embodied Intelligence
    • Biomedical Data Mining
    • Generative Materials Design

🔬 Research

  • Adaptive Contrastive Optimization for Safe and Robust Robot Manipulation: proposed ACORN, a plug-and-play contrastive optimization method for aligning robot trajectories with expert demonstrations under safety and robustness challenges.
  • Equivariant Machine Learning Force Fields and Generative Materials Design: explored diffusion models, flow matching, three-body attention, spherical harmonic convolution, and acceleration techniques for large-scale materials datasets.

🌟 Selected Projects

  • Multimodal Biomedical Pattern Mining for Disease Combination Medication: modeled joint medication pattern mining as a machine learning classification problem and built a Streamlit-based web system.
  • Intelligent Manhole Cover Hazard Recognition and Processing System: applied object detection and diffusion-based image generation to improve recognition under imbalanced samples, with a UniApp-based application.
  • Macrosomia Risk Prediction with Ensemble Learning: designed a feature selection strategy and Stacking ensemble architecture to improve model generalization and stability.

🏆 Honors

  • Outstanding Graduate of Gansu Province
  • National First Prize, 5th China Open Source Innovation Competition
  • National Second Prize, China Collegiate Computing Competition
  • Honorable Mention, MCM/ICM 2025
